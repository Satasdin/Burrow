Download Burrow.

Burrow 1.2.3: the app and the burrow program, for Macs with Apple silicon.

Install with one line opens straight away

curl -fsSL https://github.com/Satasdin/Burrow/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh -s -- --app
  1. Open Terminal (in Applications › Utilities), paste the line and press Return.
  2. It puts Burrow in Applications and the burrow program in ~/.local/bin, checks every file against its SHA-256 checksum, and offers to add ~/.local/bin to your PATH.
  3. Open Burrow from Applications. It opens without the “Open Anyway” step below: macOS asks that only of apps a browser downloaded, and this line downloads with curl.

Opening it the first time

Burrow isn't notarised by Apple (that needs a paid Apple Developer account), so the first time you open a downloaded copy, macOS stops it. You allow it once; after that it opens like any app.

  1. Open Burrow.dmg and drag Burrow onto the Applications folder in the same window. Running it from the disk image or from Downloads makes macOS run it from a hidden copy, so drag it first.
  2. Open Burrow from Applications. macOS says “Burrow” Not Opened (Apple could not verify it is free of malware). Choose Done, not Move to Trash.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security. Beside “Burrow” was blocked to protect your Mac, choose Open Anyway. The button is there for about an hour after step 2.
  4. Enter your Mac's password, then choose Open Anyway once more. Burrow opens, and from then on opens with no questions.

Or, after dragging it to Applications, one line in Terminal does steps 2 to 4: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Burrow.app
