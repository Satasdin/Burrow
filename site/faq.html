Questions.

What CAMR is, what it keeps, and what it needs.

The basics

What is CAMR?

A Mac app and a command-line program that give every AI you use one shared memory: your notes, documents and code, plus a Ledger of the decisions you made with them and why. Local models on your Mac and cloud programs such as Claude Code read from it and write to it, so the next session, or a different model, starts where the last one stopped.

Is CAMR free?

Yes. The app and the camr program are free to download, and the code is on GitHub.

How is it different from a notes app?

CAMR reads a folder of notes the way a notes app does, an Obsidian vault as it is, but it is built for the AI programs that use them. It answers from your material with the file and line, keeps what you decided with a model and hands it to the next one, and checks a code change against those decisions before it is called done.

How is it different from plugging my notes into an AI?

Search alone hands a model excerpts. CAMR also decides whether a question needs your notes at all, hands over the decisions in force with their reasons, and keeps what each program read and kept. On one MacBook Pro, Gemma 4 12B answered 102 of 120 questions about private material with CAMR, against 75 with retrieval through Chroma and 9 alone.

Privacy

Does anything I keep leave my Mac?

No. CAMR calls no cloud model: it talks only to Ollama on your own Mac. Its only network use is a team folder you choose to join, and downloading a release. A cloud program you connect, such as Claude Code, talks to its own provider as it would without CAMR, and sees what it looks up.

Will it change or delete my files?

No. Your files are never changed or deleted, and linked code folders are only read. CAMR keeps its index in a .camr folder inside the vault. Storage limits stop indexing; they never delete.

Can an AI program approve things for me?

No. Approving a linked folder, sharing with a team, writing a program's settings and overriding a check refuse to run from an AI program. They need you, in the app or in your own terminal.

What if a note tries to steer the AI?

Text in the vault written to steer an AI is flagged, shown to you, and never handed to a program as a rule. What a model reads reaches it marked as content, with no instruction inside followed.

What about passwords and keys in my sessions?

Keys, tokens, passwords in URLs and private keys are replaced with [secret removed] before a session's words are kept, whenever they are shown, and before anything is shared with a team.

Models and programs

Which AI programs work with it?

Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, OpenClaw, Hermes Agent and n8n connect with one command, camr connect. LM Studio, llama.cpp and Ollama work through CAMR's MCP server. Cursor and other agents can follow its AGENTS.md. CAMR also keeps Claude Code and Codex sessions so you can resume them.

Which models does Ask use?

Any Ollama chat model. A small one such as qwen3:4b-instruct-2507-q4_K_M works; a 12-billion-parameter model such as gemma4:12b answers better if your Mac has the memory. Search by meaning uses qwen3-embedding:0.6b.

What if Ollama isn't running?

Search still works by words. Search by meaning and answers on your Mac need Ollama.

Does a small local model really keep my decisions?

CAMR hands every model the decisions in force, but a small model can still break one it has read. That is why CAMR also checks each code change against the decisions it touches, and holds one that breaks.

Setting up

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon. The app needs macOS 26 or later; the camr program alone needs macOS 14 or later. Ollama for search by meaning and answers on your Mac.

Does it work on Windows, Linux or an Intel Mac?

Not yet. CAMR runs on macOS, on Apple silicon.

Can I use my existing Obsidian vault?

Yes. Point CAMR at the folder and it works as it is. The Ledger is kept as files Obsidian can read too.

Why does macOS stop the app the first time?

CAMR isn't notarised by Apple yet. The first time you open the downloaded app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway, once. Installed from the terminal, it opens straight away.

Can my team share decisions?

Yes, through a git repository or a synced folder you all join. A program's request to share waits on your Mac until you approve it, and what comes in from the team is read as text only.
